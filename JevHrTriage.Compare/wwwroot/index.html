The same HR triage questions go to every model. Decision models (System One API) and general chat LLMs are asked identical questions about identical requests. Their answers go through the same routing rules, and the results are scored against hand-written labels.

Mock mode: every number on this page is synthetic. No model was called. All contenders share one keyword-based mock plus random noise, and their latencies are simulated. Use this mode to try the page, not to compare models. Set Compare:Mode=Live and an API key to get real results.

Scorecard

Category acc. is the share of answers in an acceptable category. p(correct) is the average probability the model put on the right answer, so higher means more correct and better calibrated. Auto-route is the share of requests the routing rules would send without a human. Agreement is the share of requests where a model picked the same category as the reference model. Consistency is the share of requests where all repeated runs picked the same category (shown when repeats are more than 1). Latency is end-to-end from this app. $/1k is the cost per 1,000 requests; values marked ≈ are estimated from token counts.
